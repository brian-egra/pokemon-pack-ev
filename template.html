Reading it. EV ÷ cost above 1.0 means the average pack's singles are worth more than the pack at that cost. "Hits only" drops the non-foil commons, uncommons, rares and energy, so it's the value you'd actually set aside rather than bulk. Vintage rows compare against 1999–2007 MSRP, which no longer exists; read the EV itself there.
Caveats. Pull rates are sampled by TCGplayer's Authentication Center and the community, not published by The Pokémon Company, and cards within a rarity are assumed equally likely. God pack odds are the middle of wide community ranges. Prices are near-mint raw market prices; a pulled card sells for less after fees. EV is a mean: most packs return far less, and the mean is carried by rare hits.
Pull-rate estimates from thepricedex.com (built on TCGplayer and community pack-opening studies); card prices from pokemontcg.io (TCGplayer market). Built {{BUILT}}.
